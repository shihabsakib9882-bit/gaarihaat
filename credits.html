About this site and photo credits

GaariHaat is a design demo by Shihab, built for a client presentation and a portfolio. The cars, prices, inspectors, bookings and phone number are samples. No real payment is taken anywhere on this site, and bookings are saved only in your own browser.

Brand new launch prices for the BYD Seal 5, BYD Sealion 5 and MG ZS come from Bangladeshi news reports in 2026. Every other price is a believable sample, not a market quote.
